Why leave anything behind?

Weight is everything

Every kilogram costs fuel. Apollo crews left descent stages, rovers and tools so they could carry home lunar rocks.

Power runs out

Dust buries solar panels, batteries freeze, plutonium decays and propellant ends. Most machines stop this way.

No way home

No one has ever fetched an old rover. When the work is done, the cheapest choice is to leave it as a monument.
