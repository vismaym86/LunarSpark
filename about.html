About and sources

The challenge

NASA Space Apps Challenge: Abandoned but not Forgotten, Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars. Team LunarSpark built this site to tell what each machine was, why it was left behind, and what it gave us.

How to read an equipment page

Every equipment page follows the same seven chapters: overview, where it began, the mission, how it worked, what it found, why it was left behind and where it is now. A timeline and the final status close each page. Photographs and extracts from NASA's mission pages sit inside the chapter they belong to.
